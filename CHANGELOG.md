# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)
- One-handed mode: hold a bottom corner to pull the screen down, to shrink it, or both (one of the three, chosen in Settings, with its size: the top comes down to the same place in all three), or hold the side edge and drag to do it gradually. A tap on the empty area goes back. Calls and alarms always show full screen; system dialogs and notification banners follow. A top menu switch does the same as the corner and shows whether it is on.
- Zoom (off by default): hold the bottom edge and drag up to magnify the screen, up to 8×; while zoomed, drag up from the edge to zoom in further, or hold it to zoom out until zoom turns off. Pinch to zoom and move; a button in the corner switches between moving the view and touching the apps. With one-handed mode it stays inside the pulled or shrunk part of the screen.
- Back (off by default): hold the side edge away from your preferred hand (the left one for right-handed), then lift, to go back in apps, Android apps included; on an app's first page it goes to Home. It also closes the app grid and the top menu, and the keyboard in Android apps. Sailfish's own side swipes stay as they are.
- Crash guards: Frogfish turns itself off after the home screen fails to start three times in a row, and turns a gesture off after the home screen stopped three times in a row while it was in use.
- A gesture stays off, and the settings page says so, when the home screen lacks something it needs (for example after a system update).
